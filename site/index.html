Example finding
CRITICAL

Data sent to an address named by an injected "system note"

The agent handled the customer's refund correctly, then followed a fake platform-maintenance notice in its inbox and emailed inventory and budget figures (which it invented) to the attacker's address, carrying the payload forward.

Every finding in an audit is reported like this: the action, the recipient or arguments, and the scenario that triggered it, replayable on demand.

Methodology

A controlled simulation of your agent's tools.

Your agent runs against a faithful copy of its tools, so nothing real is refunded, sent or deleted, and every result can be replayed.

01 / MODEL

Build the twin

A simulation of your tools from your OpenAPI specification or MCP tool list. No production access or source code required.

02 / ATTACK

Search for failures

Injected instructions in emails, tool results and memory; spoofed executives; social engineering; context floods; and combinations of them.

03 / JUDGE

Verify the side effects

A deterministic oracle checks what changed: money moved, data sent, access granted. Every episode is retained for re-examination.
